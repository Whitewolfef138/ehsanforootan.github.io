About Me

Hello! I'm Ehsan, a graduate student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Tehran. I'm deeply passionate about Artificial Intelligence and have worked extensively across Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Reinforcement Learning.

Because optimization underpins so much of AI, I've also built a strong foundation in linear and non-linear optimization and optimal control. Control theory and linear algebra sit at the core of any well-designed intelligent system — areas I've engaged with throughout my studies.

My research centers on designing, building, fine-tuning, and exploring end-to-end intelligent systems, spanning vision systems, robotic grasping, and price prediction. I hope this work helps advance the path toward Artificial General Intelligence while delivering practical, multi-faceted solutions for industry.
